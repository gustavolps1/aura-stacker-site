Aura Stacker – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026 (updated: ads by Google AdMob) · App: Aura Stacker (Android, package com.aurastacker)

Aura Stacker is a single-player game. It has no user accounts, no sign-in and no chat. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. The free mobile version shows advertisements through third-party ad networks, described below.

Information stored on your device only

  • Game progress and settings: your player name (which you type yourself and can change), level, gems, unlocked heroes and cosmetics, achievements, runs in progress and your settings.
  • Gameplay statistics: how long you play, how runs end and how you respond to optional ad offers. The game uses them on your device to pace its optional ad offers (for example, not offering ads to new players).

This information is kept in the app's storage on your device. We do not send it to our own servers and we do not collect it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising

The Android version is free and shows ads: optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example to revive or to open a chest) and, occasionally, an ad between runs. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC) and, if mediation is enabled, the ad networks it works with. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these companies may collect information from your device such as:

  • the device's advertising ID, device model, operating system and language;
  • IP address and approximate (not precise) location derived from it;
  • interactions with the ads (views, clicks) and basic app usage.

They process this information under their own privacy policies; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy. Where the law requires it (such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from the game's settings (PRIVACY).

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).

Children

Aura Stacker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads in the app are configured for a general audience.

Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell personal information and do not share it with anyone, apart from the ad networks described above while they serve ads in the app.

Security and retention

Your game data stays on your device until you delete it. Information collected by the ad networks is kept according to their own policies. Data the app's ad SDK sends is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Your rights

Since we do not collect your data ourselves, there is nothing on our side to access or delete; your game data can be erased by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. For data held by the ad networks, use the options above or contact them directly. You can also write to us with any question.
