Aura Stacker – Delete Your Data

Updated: 16 October 2026 · App: Aura Stacker (Android, package com.aurastacker)

Aura Stacker has no accounts of its own and keeps no player data on our servers. Your game data is in two places: on your device, and (if you sign in to Google Play Games) as a saved game in your Play Games account. You can delete both yourself, at any time, with the steps below. See also the privacy policy.

1. Game data on your device

Your level, gems, heroes, cosmetics, achievements, runs in progress, settings and gameplay statistics.

  1. Open your phone's Settings → Apps → Aura Stacker.
  2. Tap Storage (or Storage & cache) → Clear storage (or Clear data).

Uninstalling the app deletes the same data. It is removed at once and cannot be recovered.

2. Your saved game in Google Play Games

The copy of your progress (level, gems, unlocked heroes and cosmetics, achievements, lifetime totals and your Play Games nickname) that lets you continue on a new phone.

  1. Open the Google Play Games app (or the Play Games section of the Play Store) → your profile → Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Play Games account & data, then under Delete individual game data find Aura Stacker and tap Delete.

Google deletes it from your account. Do step 1 too, or the game will copy your device's progress back the next time it starts signed in. Your Play Games profile itself is Google's and is managed in the same settings.

3. Advertising data

The free Android version shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device information such as the advertising ID (see the privacy policy). We do not hold this data. To delete or reset your advertising ID: phone Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) → Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID. Google's own data is managed at your Google Account's Data & privacy page.

What is kept

Nothing on our side: we have no server copy of your data to keep or delete. Data held by Google (Play Games, ads) follows Google's privacy policy.
